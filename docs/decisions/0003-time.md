# 0003 Time: a life from birth to death, estimated ends, organisations, events

Status: accepted (made autonomously 2026-10-25, to be reviewed with Kai)
Date: 2026-10-25

## Context

The map shows a dot when, in the selected time, a person lives or something happens. The entity pages give years of
birth and death (normalised, `jl:birthDateNormalized`, else a date that starts with a year); often only one.
Organisations have years of establishment and termination in the GND, often only the first.

## Decision

- A person is shown at all their places (birth, activity and exile from the GND, death, burial) for the whole life,
  from the year of birth to the year of death. With only one of the two years, the life is taken to last 70 years
  (capped at 2026) and marked as estimated. A life of more than 110 years is an error in the data (98 people on
  2026-10-25, e.g. born "1054", buried 1894): the year of birth is not used. Death before birth: left out.
- A second mode, "births and deaths in their year", counts the places of birth, death and burial only in that
  year (and not at all when that year is estimated); places of activity and exile still count for the whole life.
- An organisation is shown at its seat from its establishment to its termination; without a termination only in the
  year of its establishment (an open end would show dissolved organisations to this day).
- A book is shown at its place of printing in its year (as derived in jlma-haskala 0005), later editions at their
  places in their years; a periodical at its place for the years of its publication period.
- The timeline counts per decade the entities of the area (as the map, but for all times); 1000–1500 is compressed,
  the years before 1000 are one bin ("earlier").

## Consequences

The estimates are visible (marked in the panel); the mode switch shows births and deaths as events.
